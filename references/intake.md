# ITR-3 consolidated intake

Use this before operating the portal/utility or editing schedules. Inventory files first, summarize established facts, and ask only unanswered applicable questions. Prefer one structured-choice round plus one structured factual response over serial yes/no prompts. Do not request PAN, Aadhaar, passwords, OTPs, or full bank credentials in chat.

## Structured-choice prompt policy

- When a structured-choice prompt capability is available, call it once upfront with one to three short decision questions. In Codex, use `request_user_input`. Use it for choices, not for amounts, identifiers, explanations, or a long checklist.
- Put the recommended option first and label it “(Recommended)”. Provide two or three mutually exclusive options per question. The client supplies a free-form Other option; do not add one.
- Prefer these three question IDs:
  - `stopping_point`: **Reach Verification page (Recommended)**, **Schedules confirmed**, or **Inspect only**. Reaching Verification does not include declaration/signing, e-verification, or submission.
  - `routine_scope`: **Full reversible scope (Recommended)**, **Entries only**, or **Review only**. Full reversible scope covers downloading missing tax-portal and broker evidence, supported entries, empty/inapplicable schedule cleanup, schedule confirmation, dependency recalculation, and supported validation-error corrections.
  - `pay_later`: **Allow Pay Later (Recommended)**, **Stop at tax result**, or **Decide later**. Pay Later only permits reaching Verification without making payment.
- Do not use auto-resolution for authorization or material tax facts; wait for the user's answer.
- If fewer than three choices remain unresolved, ask only those. Do not re-ask facts or permissions already established.
- After the structured-choice response, ask all remaining factual/free-form questions in one structured message. If that capability is unavailable, include the same three choices in that message.
- Use at most one further structured-choice round for a genuinely new conditional decision. Group all exceptions rather than prompting schedule by schedule.

## Conversation-state ledger

Before asking questions—especially after a pause, compaction, login handoff, or resumed browser turn—reconstruct this ledger from the conversation and evidence:

- filing year, taxpayer status, residence, regime and return type;
- confirmed income sources and confirmed negative facts;
- evidence present, downloaded, verified, stale or still missing;
- authorized reversible actions, stopping point and Pay-Later choice;
- schedules already reviewed, retained, removed or confirmed;
- current portal/utility page and validation level;
- unresolved facts and separately gated actions.

Do not convert a missing ledger entry into a negative fact. Do not re-ask a ledger entry that the user already answered clearly. A terse answer such as “yes” applies only to the immediately preceding explicit question; record the resolved fact in plain language so it does not become ambiguous after resumption.

## Evidence readiness

Inspect the current working directory read-only. Classify likely evidence by filename, type, modification time, and—where safe—content:

- AIS/TIS/26AS or prefill JSON;
- Form 16/16A, challans, and tax-credit evidence;
- Form 64B/business-trust or AIF statements;
- broker tax P&L, trade-level capital-gains report, ledger/other-debits report, and contract notes;
- prior return/acknowledgement;
- JSON generated by the offline utility.

Report what appears present, duplicated, missing, or ambiguous. Ask which duplicate is authoritative only when modification time and content do not resolve it. Never treat generated upload JSON as prefill source data.

When the online portal is in scope, include missing official-evidence downloads in the routine-scope choice instead of asking permission file by file. Download only the conditional set in `portal-downloads.md`. CAPTCHA, OTP, Aadhaar authentication, and credentials remain user-only steps.

When Zerodha transactions are in scope and broker evidence is missing, include the necessary Console downloads in the same routine-scope choice. Open `https://console.zerodha.com/`, ask the user to log in immediately, and resume only after confirmation. Never handle the user's credentials, PIN, password, OTP, or TOTP.

## Consolidated factual questionnaire

Ask the following applicable items together. Replace questions with a “known facts—correct if wrong” summary whenever the answer is already established.

Keep the actual message short enough to answer. Ask only facts that remain unresolved and can change ITR applicability, a schedule, tax computation, validation, or the authorized stopping point. Do not paste every item below when most are irrelevant. Bundle negative-scope facts into one correction-oriented statement, for example: “I currently have no evidence of firm partnership, directorship/unlisted shares, foreign assets or income, VDA, brought-forward losses, or Portuguese community-property treatment; list any exception.” Absence of evidence is not confirmation—obtain the user's correction/confirmation once, then retain it in the ledger.

### Filing posture

1. State the AY/FY or Tax Year inferred from today's India date using `tax-year-selection.md`; ask the user to correct it if the filing is revised, belated, updated, historical, notice-driven, or otherwise not the ordinary current filing. Also confirm individual status, residential status and statutory residence condition.
2. Original/revised/belated/updated return and intended filing section/due-date treatment.
3. New or old regime; prior/current Form 10-IEA facts where applicable.
4. Confirm ITR-3 and disclose partnership-firm status, directorship, unlisted shares, ESOP/deferred tax, agricultural income, foreign assets/accounts/signing authority/income/credit, and other facts affecting schedule selection.
5. Whether prefill/AIS is already imported, the current generated-JSON baseline, and files that must not be used.

### Business/profession

1. Activity and profession/nature code; each distinct business activity actually carried on.
2. Gross receipts split between banking/electronic, cash, and other modes.
3. Whether section 44ADA applies and the amount declared (at least the statutory minimum when required).
4. Books maintained, section 44AA position, tax-audit applicability/performance, and applicable audit forms.
5. If no books: 31 March sundry debtors, sundry creditors, stock-in-trade, and physical cash. Require factual values; an omitted JSON field is not zero.
6. Any non-presumptive business, speculation, intraday/F&O, VDA/crypto, or partnership remuneration/interest.

### Investments and other income

1. Sales/redemptions of shares, equity/debt funds, property, gold, unlisted/foreign assets, or other capital assets; brought-forward losses.
2. Broker/CAMS/KFintech evidence and whether eligible transfer expenses should be independently reconciled.
3. Form 64B/PTI sources, taxable components, exempt components, section 56(2)(xii) or return-of-capital components, and related TDS.
4. Savings/term-deposit interest, dividends, dividend receipt-date/quarter data, family pension, gifts, winnings, rent, house property, and any other income.
5. Deductions still allowed under the chosen regime; do not solicit old-regime-only claims for a new-regime return.
6. Advance/self-assessment tax, TDS/TCS, and whether credits are claimed now or carried forward.

### Banks and verification facts

1. Which prefilled Indian accounts were active and should be retained; preferred validated refund account.
2. Whether masked account details, type, IFSC, and validation status are correct. Request missing bank particulars only through the safest available UI.
3. Intended filing address and verification place spelling.

### Authorization fallback when the tool is unavailable

Ask once, in the same message:

> Choose a stopping point: inspect only, confirm all applicable schedules, or reach the Verification page without signing. Choose routine scope: review only, evidence-supported entries only, or full reversible scope including downloads of missing tax-portal and broker evidence, schedule cleanup, and supported validation corrections. If tax is payable, should I stop at the result, decide later, or select Pay Later solely to reach Verification without paying? Payment, declaration/signing, upload, e-verification, and submission will still require separate action-time confirmation.

Accept a clear combined authorization such as “full reversible scope to the Verification page, with Pay Later.” Do not re-ask for each schedule or validation correction within that scope.

Treat authorizations cumulatively. For example, if the user has already said “remove all unnecessary schedules,” “proceed,” and “continue until Verification,” record schedule cleanup and the stopping point as authorized rather than asking for a new yes/no at each stage. A later narrower instruction overrides the ledger; ordinary progress messages do not erase earlier authorization.

## Conditional second round

Use at most one focused follow-up round for issues revealed by the evidence, such as:

- conflicting broker and generated-JSON totals;
- unexplained Form 64B components or section 56 amounts;
- unknown no-books closing balances;
- ambiguous residency/foreign-asset facts;
- a populated schedule that appears inapplicable;
- a new tax payable/refund outcome that changes the requested stopping point.

Group all known exceptions into that round. Do not interrupt after every schedule.

## Intake summary before editing

Present:

- **Filing:** AY, status, residence, regime, return type, Form 10-IEA, authorized stopping point
- **Profession:** activity/code, receipts by mode, 44ADA, books/audit, no-books balances
- **Investments/PTI:** assets, evidence, expenses, losses, Form 64B components
- **Other income/deductions:** categories, timing evidence, chosen-regime treatment
- **Tax credits:** TDS/TCS/challans and claimed/carry-forward treatment
- **Banks/verification:** retained/refund account masked, place
- **Routine authorization:** allowed actions
- **Unresolved facts:** explicit list or `none`

Ask the user to correct the consolidated summary once. Proceed without further routine confirmation when the required facts and authorization are complete.
